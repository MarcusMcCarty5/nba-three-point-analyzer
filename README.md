# NBA Three-Point Strategy Analyzer

## Project Overview

The NBA Three-Point Strategy Analyzer is a Python project that evaluates the effectiveness of three-point shooting on both the offensive and defensive sides of basketball. Using NBA team statistics, the program will analyze how three-point production and three-point defense relate to team success and winning percentage. The goal is to determine whether offensive three-point shooting or defensive three-point prevention has a stronger relationship with winning.

## MVP (Version 1.0)

The first version of this project will focus on collecting and analyzing NBA team statistics to compare offensive and defensive three-point performance.

The MVP will include:

- Collecting NBA team statistics through the NBA Stats CSV Files.
- Retrieving three-point attempts, makes, percentages, and winning percentages.
- Organizing and storing statistics in CSV files.
- Cleaning and preparing data using Pandas.
- Analyzing the relationship between offensive three-point performance and winning percentage.
- Analyzing the relationship between defensive three-point performance and winning percentage.
- Comparing offensive and defensive results to determine which has a stronger relationship with winning.
- Creating graphs and visualizations using Matplotlib.

## Future Plans (Version 2.0+)

After completing the MVP, I may expand the project with the following features:

- **Individual Player Analysis:** Incorporate individual player statistics to evaluate how specific players impact team success.
- **Multiple Season Analysis:** Analyze multiple NBA seasons to identify changes in three-point strategies over time.
- **Additional Visualizations:** Create more graphs to make statistical trends easier to understand.
- **Team Strategy Comparisons:** Compare teams based on different three-point strategies and playing styles.
